Submit

Submissions are reviewed by the editorial team. You can submit with your real name or a pen name. Anonymous submissions are accepted.

How to submit

Email your piece to: quietink.submissions@gmail.com (replace this with your real email)

Include in your email

  • Your name (or pen name)
  • Year level / age range (optional)
  • Title + genre
  • Your piece (in the email or attached as Google Doc)
  • One sentence: “I give permission for Quiet Ink to publish this.”

Guidelines

  • Original work only
  • 300–1,500 words (flexible)
  • No hate speech; keep it respectful
  • Content warnings appreciated if needed

Prefer a form? Create a Google Form and paste the link here as a big button.

About

Quiet Ink exists to make publishing feel possible. Not for “perfect” writers — for real ones. We curate youth writing so readers can find pieces that hit, linger, and mean something.

Privacy

Pen names are welcome. If you choose anonymity, we’ll keep your identity private. Editors may also use pen names.

Editorial note

We edit lightly for clarity (with permission). We don’t change your voice.
